Model Accuracy

Our model has been trained and tested on real-world car sales data. The model achieves high prediction accuracy for most typical car configurations.

However, please note that predictions are estimates based on historical data and market trends. Actual selling prices may vary based on factors not captured by the model, such as:

  • Car condition (damage, wear and tear)
  • Special features or modifications
  • Local market conditions
  • Seasonal demand variations
  • Color and aesthetic preferences

We continuously improve our model with new data to provide the most accurate predictions possible.
